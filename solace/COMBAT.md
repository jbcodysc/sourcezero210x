# Solace party combat

The existing encounter model now stores `battle.party`, a generic list with independent HP, stats, gear, guard and temporary status. The original hero-facing accessors remain for older encounters and tests. `progress.party` holds persistent companions; `createLou`, `joinLou`, `normalizeParty`, `carryParty`, `awardParty`, and `restoreParty` live in `city/party.mjs`.

Each standing member chooses an action and an enemy target before a round resolves. Goods additionally choose a party recipient; the shared inventory reserves a selected item so one remaining meal cannot be assigned twice. Guard and healing keep their global first-priority rule. Every member and living enemy receives one speed-ordered turn, and reinforcement arrival still consumes its first turn. Downed members are skipped; defeat requires all members to fall. Ordinary food can restore a downed ally to action. Clinics restore the entire party. XP updates each member independently, using the shared level curve and sequential level-up notices.

Lou joins at the protagonist’s level: 140 + 14 per level maximum HP, 55 + 3 per level Attack including his halberd, 14 + 2 per level Defense including workwear, and 3 + level Speed. He has no magic capability or MP. Equipment names and stats are saved explicitly for future party expansion.

The Series 8 containment miniboss learns repeated attacks per character, reducing repeated damage gradually by at most 30%. Guard or a healing action resets that character’s repetition. Restraint foam reduces one member’s speed for two following rounds. Charged containment attacks still occur on the promised next turn. It uses stun pulses and building restraint systems, and does not call reinforcements. The story handles its failed containment and readable mission cache after victory.

Solace battle returns reuse the existing BattleScene with `state.origin.scene='Solace'`. Ephemeral `onBattleWon(progress,encounter)` and `onBattleLost(progress,encounter)` callbacks hand story events back to the Solace scene. Defeat returns to the local clinic; ordinary victory/escape return to the originating map. Scripted recovery attack sets `encounter.noRun=true`.

## Enemy assets

Built-in image generation created `solace/assets/residential-enemies.png` (1024×1536 RGBA). `enemy-art.mjs` registers separately framed map and battle views of a residential cleaner, luggage porter, building maintenance unit and slim Series 8 containment android. Cenexis security and recovery drones intentionally retain the Chapter 2 enemy-family visuals. No source image is required outside the repository.
