# S.C.R.A.P. encounter update

The original `argus` enemy ID, save flags, music keys and asset IDs are retained for save compatibility. Public names are controlled by `fairmont/security-identity.mjs`.

## Combat

S.C.R.A.P.’s regular and charged attacks deal 60% more damage than his prior tuning and takes half incoming damage, rounded to whole HP. A 120-damage hit becomes 60; the level-26 resonant vibrosword deals 63 instead of 126. Regular and charged attacks retain their existing defense and Guard calculations before the outgoing boost. Other enemies are unchanged.

- Missile Volley replaces his next action at or below 20% HP, once per encounter, with the requested taunt on the same turn. It also supersedes a pending charged attack at this threshold.
- Original supplied transparent sheet: `city/assets/effects/argus_missile_volley_fx_sheet.png`, 7176×4740, six columns, 36 frames of 1196×790 at 18 FPS. Loaded only for this boss. The test desktop supports a 16384-pixel maximum texture.
- `MissileVolleyEffect` reuses the live screen-attack renderer and battle input lock. Three once-only impact callbacks at frames 23, 27 and 31; no damage at launch. Launch cues occur at 0, 5, 10 and 15. Camera shake and pale flashes are generated by Phaser/the live overlay.
- Fixed 295 total damage (98 + 98 + 99), exempt from SCRAP’s outgoing damage boost, independent of level, defense, and ordinary enemy damage scaling; Guard reduces it to 89. The result reads “A missile volley was fired, doing massive X damage!” Existing 5% miss rate is retained. The chance of a silly turn remains 5%; reinforcements remain disabled.
- Lethal damage holds the boss at 1 HP and enters `bossRetreat`. Combat commands and rolling damage pause for his farewell. XP and credits are granted once after the final line; existing level-up notices remain intact.

## Story and recovery

The introduction resolves [Classified] → expanded designation → the protagonist’s “So, you're S.C.R.A.P.?” → angry denial under S.C.R.A.P. All subsequent speech uses the revealed name. Existing broadcasts and resonance dialogue remain in place.

Victory sets `CH2_ARGUS_ESCAPE_PENDING`. The scene turns and fires, saves `CH2_ARGUS_WALL_BREACHED`, then flies through the opening and shrinks away. `CH2_ARGUS_ESCAPE_SEEN` clears the pending escape. Reload before the blast replays the short exit; reload after the blast resumes flight without another shot. Afterward the existing resonance reflection and archive progression continue. The breach persists as scenery without becoming an accessible shortcut.

Scene shutdown cancels effects, timers and tweens. The escape uses the existing silent entrance audio mode and music router. Older completed saves do not replay a newly added exit, while older pending boss saves still resume combat normally.
