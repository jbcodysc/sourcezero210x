# Clinic and textile artwork

These standalone PNGs were produced with the built-in image generator and copied
unchanged into `fairmont/assets/interior-v2/`. Generation stopped when the user
asked to continue without further image generation. Source alpha is preserved.
Runtime frames trim transparent outside margins without modifying source pixels.

- `clinic-bed.png`: adjustable mint vinyl examination bed, metal side rails,
  scissor lift, medical paper, and locking casters.
- `clinic-cabinet.png`: glazed ivory medical cabinet containing gauze, bottles,
  and bandages, with real doors, hinges, locks, and handles.
- `clinic-cart.png`: steel diagnostic trolley with monitor, cuff, dressings, and
  organized supplies.
- `clinic-screen.png`: pleated medical privacy screen on hinged tubular frames.
- `woven-rug.png`: detailed navy woven rug with tan border and thread texture.

Art direction: rich illustrated pixel art, dark navy/plum contours, warm
highlights and cool shadows, three-quarter overhead RPG furniture views,
transparent cutouts. The supplied `Art direction v1.0.png` was visually inspected
and used to describe the established style. No new character or enemy art changes.

Exact generation prompts are in `clinic-interior-prompts.json`.
